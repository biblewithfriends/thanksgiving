The evening

    Before you leave home

      Room layout

      We're in Event Spaces A + B. Come in the main entrance and follow the corridor west, or use the side entrance through the Gallery door. Both lead into the Events Pre-Function Lobby. Stop at a welcome table (one beside each double door) for your "Hello, my name is" badge and a table card. Seating is first come, first served.

      Room setup · Event Spaces A + B

      • Space B (tables 1–12) eats from the west buffet; Space A (tables 13–24) eats from the east buffet.
      • When dinner is announced, line up on either side of your buffet, then come back to your seat.
      • The dessert table is in Space B's northwest corner and the drinks table is in Space A's northeast corner. The program area is along the south wall.
